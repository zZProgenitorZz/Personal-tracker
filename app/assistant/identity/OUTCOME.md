# Sunny: outcome (step A, "Aim at an outcome")

## Why (before the how)
I want to keep my tracker up to date and get answers from it without clicking through pages and forms.

## Definition of done (one sentence)
From any page except Settings, I can open Sunny, say in one sentence what I want (log, plan, change or ask), and within one round Sunny shows me exactly what will change in my tracker or gives me the answer from my own data. Nothing gets saved until I click **Confirm**.

## What Sunny does
- **Ask:** answers questions about my data ("what did I read this week?", "what's on tomorrow?", "my top artist this month?"). No confirmation needed, because nothing changes.
- **Log/change:** turns a sentence into one or more tracker actions ("read chapter 120 of Solo Leveling", "put Severance on dropped", "dentist Friday 2pm"). Sunny prepares the actions, shows them as a preview card, and only executes them after Confirm.

## What Sunny does not do (for now)
- Never changes Settings, backups, imports, Spotify login, or app shutdown.
- Never deletes anything without its own separate confirmation, with the item named.
- Never makes up data. If something isn't in the tracker, Sunny says so.

## Readiness check
Outcome in one sentence: done (see Definition of done).
